# First Steps with Shog

Building a portfolio in the terminal changes the shape of the problem.

Instead of fighting for visual novelty through another grid of cards, the interface can lean on what terminals are already good at: focus, rhythm, and text that earns attention.

## Why this format works

- It is small enough to feel intentional.
- It makes interaction part of the presentation.
- It keeps the writing and project details in the foreground.

## What comes next

The next step is turning the rough scaffold into something personal: real project content, real writing, and a visual language that feels owned rather than borrowed.
